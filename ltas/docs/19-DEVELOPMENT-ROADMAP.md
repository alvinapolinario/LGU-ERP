# 19 — Development roadmap

This is a dependency-driven delivery plan for a small team. Dates, budget, staffing, procurement and milestone durations are **TBD** after workshops and backlog estimation. Initial staffing assumption: a product/secretariat lead, a small full-stack team, shared QA/IT support and part-time legal/privacy/records reviewers; do not convert this assumption into a resource commitment.

Internal lifecycle MVP candidate = Phases 1–6 with minimum searchable metadata, documents, audit and reminders throughout. Phases 7–9 add archive/codification depth, mature reporting and public access. No real-data production release bypasses Phase 10 gates, even if a limited internal pilot launches earlier. A pilot can use synthetic data while policy decisions remain pending.

## Sequencing refinements

Audit, authentication, test fixtures, source/version integrity, backup design and threat modeling start in P1. Basic dashboard/list search and operational reminders begin in P2, not only P8. Scanner selection precedes real uploads. Public release controls are designed in P2 document classification even though the portal arrives in P9. Historical import begins with a source inventory in P0 and a staged pilot in P7; full migration waits for reconciliation and retention approval.

No phase needs microservices, OpenSearch, AI, remote voting or advanced document diff to meet the proposed MVP. Limit workflow configuration to reviewed templates rather than a visual programming tool. Use feature flags for unfinished capabilities, but never to bypass policy or immutability.

Each phase ends with a demonstration using linked requirement/test IDs, documented limitations and updated decisions/risks. Product ownership approves scope; legal/records/privacy owners approve policy matters; IT validates operability. See [traceability](27-TRACEABILITY-MATRIX.md) and [tests](18-TESTING-STRATEGY.md).
